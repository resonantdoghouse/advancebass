## Practice Tips

This exercise runs through all seven diatonic seventh chords in the key of **C Major**, ascending and descending through each chord scale position continuously up the neck and back down without jumping octaves.

### The Harmonic Progression

| Bar | Degree | Chord | Direction | Notes | Fretboard Zone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **I** | **Cmaj7** | Ascend (1-3-5-7) | C – E – G – B | Frets 2–5 (Low A & D strings) |
| **1** | **ii** | **Dm7** | Descend (7-5-3-1) | C – A – F – D | Frets 2–5 (Low G, D, A strings) |
| **2** | **iii** | **Em7** | Ascend (1-3-5-7) | E – G – B – D | Frets 5–9 (Mid A, D, G strings) |
| **2** | **IV** | **Fmaj7** | Descend (7-5-3-1) | E – C – A – F | Frets 5–9 (Mid G, D, A strings) |
| **3** | **V** | **G7** | Ascend (1-3-5-7) | G – B – D – F | Frets 7–10 (Upper A, D, G strings) |
| **3** | **vi** | **Am7** | Descend (7-5-3-1) | G – E – C – A | Frets 9–12 (Upper G, D, A strings) |
| **4** | **vii°** | **Bm7b5** | Ascend (1-3-5-7) | B – D – F – A | Frets 9–14 (High D & G strings) |
| **4** | **VIII** | **Cmaj7** | Descend (7-5-3-1) | B – G – E – C | Frets 12–16 (High G, D, A strings) |

Bars 5–8 reverse the cycle from the top position, cascading and sweeping back down the scale to resolve on low C2.

---

### How to Practise

- **Start at 60 BPM with a Metronome**: Focus on even rhythmic spacing and clean string crossing before pushing to the target 80 BPM.
- **Observe the Step-Wise Pivots**: Notice how the final note of each ascending arpeggio and the starting note of the descending arpeggio are always separated by just a half-step or whole-step on adjacent frets.
- **Maintain Left-Hand Position Discipline**: Keep your fretting hand relaxed as you shift from the 2nd fret all the way up to the 16th fret.
- **Say the Chord Tones Aloud**: Speak the interval names (*"Root, 3rd, 5th, 7th"*) as you play each note to reinforce fretboard geometry and ear training.
